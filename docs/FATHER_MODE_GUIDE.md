# Father Mode Guide

**Father Mode** translates raw derivative metrics, volatility anomalies, and price candles into simplified, reassuring, and objective Hinglish summaries for the user's father.

## Prompt Construction

The AI reasoning engine uses custom system prompt constraints to enforce Hinglish outputs styled under exactly five headers:

1. **Kya Hua? (What happened?):** Translates trend lines and indicators into simple language.
2. **Kyun Farq Padta Hai? (Why it matters?):** Details direct impact on invested portfolio capital.
3. **Kya Risk/Nuksaan Ho Sakta Hai? (What is the risk?):** Outlines stop-loss levels and volatility zones.
4. **Mujhe Kya Karna Chahiye? (Recommended action?):** Suggests BUY, HOLD, REDUCE, or SELL simply.
5. **Aage Nazar Kahan Rakhein? (What to watch next?):** Identifies support and resistance zones.

No complex derivative math or equations are presented, ensuring simple and safe decision support.
