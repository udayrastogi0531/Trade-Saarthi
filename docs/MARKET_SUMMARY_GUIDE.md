# Market Summary Guide

The Market Summary Engine provides a three-interval briefing workflow to keep investors oriented throughout the trading day.

## Briefing Intervals

1. **🌅 Pre-Market Briefing:**
   * **Timing:** Before market open (08:30 AM).
   * **Metrics:** Global indexes, crude oil futures, currency rates, FII/DII net flows, and watchlist breakout opportunities.
2. **⚡ Intraday Briefing:**
   * **Timing:** During market hours (12:00 PM).
   * **Metrics:** Real-time volume spikes, volatile sector movements, and high-risk flags.
3. **🌆 Closing Briefing:**
   * **Timing:** After market close (04:00 PM).
   * **Metrics:** Settle prices, daily gainers, overnight stop-loss recommendations, and decay indicators.
