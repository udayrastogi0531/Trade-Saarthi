# Portfolio Manager Guide

This guide details the operational manual and design guidelines for the **AI Portfolio Manager & Market Intelligence Platform (v4.0.2+)**.

## Core Workstation Concepts

The platform is designed specifically as a father-friendly market intelligence terminal, shifting the focus from highly complex, intimidating charts to simplified decision indicators:

1. **Portfolio Synchronization:** Direct imports of holdings and net positions from Zerodha Kite (with fully automated real-time fallback to Yahoo Finance and mock portfolios).
2. **Quantitative Health Scoring:** Scoring stock quality on a simple 0-100 scale.
3. **Decisive Directives:** Standardizing holding decisions into **BUY**, **HOLD**, **REDUCE**, and **SELL** badges with comprehensive explanations.
4. **Conversational spoken briefings:** Creating audibly readable, plain Hinglish/Hindi briefings (Pre-market, Intraday, Closing) and system breakouts alerts.
